Pay & levels
How pay grows with your level
Every track moves through the same bands. As you take on more ownership, both your level and your pay range step up. Ranges reflect the current GFF payscale and don't include the benefits box, perks, or commissions on top.
Director / Vice President
$72K+ salary
Director of Paid Search · Director of Social Media · and other department-leading roles.
Manager / Strategist
$19–$35 /hr
General Manager · Strategist I, II, III · Project Manager · Development Manager · Client Success Manager · Social Media Manager.
Team Member
$14–$23 /hr
Digital Marketing Specialist I, II, III · SEO / Content Specialist · Graphic Designer I, II, III · Photo / Video Specialist · Client Success Specialist · Executive Admin.
Intern / Work Release
Where most people start — learning the tools and supporting the team on live work.
